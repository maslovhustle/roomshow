Point a camera at the room, restyle it live, project it back. Runs entirely in the browser.

Open the stage on the machine wired to the projector, then open the remote on your phone with the same code.

Cross-device remote (Supabase, free tier)

Without keys the remote only reaches the stage inside the same browser — fine for testing, useless in a room. Paste a free Supabase project's URL and anon key to control the stage from your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere else; the keys stay in this browser's localStorage.
